About the company

A small, local crew that answers the phone.

Alpine Plumbing & Heating LLC has operated in Laramie since April 2021, led by Jeremiah Jensen. What started as a small operation has grown into a team of more than 20 technicians serving homes across Laramie and the surrounding area.

Homeowners come back to Alpine for the same reasons: crews show up when they say they will, explain the problem in plain terms, and charge a fair, honest price — whether it's a dripping faucet or a full boiler replacement in the middle of a Wyoming winter.
